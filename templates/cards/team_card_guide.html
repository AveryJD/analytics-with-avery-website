Learn More About Each Section

Hover over or click/tap any part of the card to see what it means.

Standings

The team's standing placement for the given season. Their record (wins-losses-overtime/shootout losses), standing points, point percentage (points earned divided by points available), their rank in their division, and their rank league wide.

Season Stats

Stats from the given season only, in all situations. The team's goals and expected goals for and against per game (GF/GP, GA/GP, xGF/GP, xGA/GP), and its 5v5 expected goal share (xGF%, the share of 5v5 expected goals the team created).

Elo Rating

The team's Elo rating after each game of the season. The rating updates after every game based on how the result compared to what was expected. To see how Elo is calculated, check out the methodologies page.

SRS Ranking

The team's league rank in the Simple Rating System (SRS) in each of the last five seasons. SRS rates a team by how many goals per game better or worse than an average team it was, adjusted for the opponents it played and for home ice. To see how SRS is calculated, check out the methodologies page.

xGoals For

The team's expected goals for (xGF) at 5v5, rated per 60 minutes and for the given season only (not weighted). It's the team's total chance of scoring on their own unblocked shot attempts, based on where and how each was taken, measuring the quality of their shots. To see how xG is calculated, check out the methodologies page.

Goals For

The team's goals scored at 5v5, rated per 60 minutes and for the given season only (not weighted).

Shots For

The team's shots on goal at 5v5, rated per 60 minutes and for the given season only (not weighted). Shots on goal include goals and saved shots, but not missed shots or blocked shots.

Shooting %

The team's shooting percentage (SH%) at 5v5 for the given season only (not weighted). Shooting percentage is calculated as goals divided by shots on goal.

Penalty Minutes Drawn

The minutes of the minor and major penalties the other team took at 5v5 against this team (misconducts and fighting majors are not counted, since neither gives a power play), which give this team a power play, rated per 60 minutes and for the given season only (not weighted).

xGoals Against

The team's expected goals against (xGA) at 5v5, rated per 60 minutes and for the given season only (not weighted). It's the total chance of scoring on the unblocked shot attempts the team allowed, measuring the quality of the shots they allowed. To see how xG is calculated, check out the methodologies page.

Goals Against

The team's goals allowed at 5v5, rated per 60 minutes and for the given season only (not weighted).

Shots Against

The team's shots on goal allowed at 5v5, rated per 60 minutes and for the given season only (not weighted).

Save %

The team's save percentage (SV%) at 5v5 for the given season only (not weighted). Save percentage is calculated as saves divided by shots on goal.

Penalty Minutes Taken

The minutes of the minor and major penalties the team took at 5v5 (misconducts and fighting majors are not counted, since neither gives a power play), which give the other team a power play, rated per 60 minutes and for the given season only (not weighted).

5v4 xGoals For

The team's expected goals for (xGF) at 5v4, rated per 2 minutes of 5v4 time for the given season only (not weighted). To see how xG is calculated, check out the methodologies page.

5v4 Goals For

The team's goals scored at 5v4, rated per 2 minutes of 5v4 time for the given season only (not weighted).

4v5 xGoals Against

The team's expected goals against (xGA) on the at 4v5, rated per 2 minutes of 4v5 time for the given season only (not weighted). To see how xG is calculated, check out the methodologies page.

4v5 Goals Against

The team's goals allowed at 4v5, rated per 2 minutes of 4v5 time for the given season only (not weighted).

How to Read a Percentile Bar

Every stat has a name (the text on the left), the team's value (the number on the right), and a percentile bar. The percentile bar shows how the team ranks among all teams that season. The number on the bar is the percentile, which is the percentage of teams that the team ranks higher than. Stats where less is better, such as goals against, are flipped so a high percentile is always good.
