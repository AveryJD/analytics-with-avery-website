Profile

Information about who the player is. The position they play and their handedness, their age (on September 1 of the given season's first year) and their birthday, their height (in feet and inches) and weight (in pounds), their draft position and year (or Undrafted), and birth country.

Season Stats

The player's stats from the given season only, in all situations (not weighted). Their games played (GP), ice time per game (TOI/GP), goals-assists-points, individual expected goals (xG), and wins above replacement (WAR). These are different from the weighted numbers below.

Overall WAR

The player's total wins above replacement (WAR), for all situations, rated per 82 games (or 84 from 2026-2027 onwards) and weighted over the past 3 seasons. It is the sum of the six components on the right. The graph below is the overall WAR percentile in each of the last five seasons, using that season alone (not weighted), with the logos showing the team(s) the player played for. A season without a dot means the player did not qualify. To see how xG, RAPM, and WAR are calculated, check out the methodologies page.

ES Offense

The player's even strength (5v5, 4v4, and 3v3) offensive WAR, rated per 82 games (or 84 from 2026-2027 onwards) and weighted over the past 3 seasons. It's the player's impact on their team's expected goals for, adjusted for teammates and opponents using regularized adjusted plus-minus (RAPM). The small graph under it is the player's Even Strength Offense WAR percentile in each of the last five seasons (with the same y-axis grid lines as Overall WAR). To see how xG, RAPM, and WAR are calculated, check out the methodologies page.

ES Defense

The player's even strength (5v5, 4v4, and 3v3) defensive WAR, rated per 82 games (or 84 from 2026-2027 onwards) and weighted over the past 3 seasons. It's the player's impact on their team's expected goals against, adjusted for teammates and opponents using regularized adjusted plus-minus (RAPM). The small graph under it is the player's Even Strength Defense WAR percentile in each of the last five seasons (with the same y-axis grid lines as Overall WAR). To see how xG, RAPM, and WAR are calculated, check out the methodologies page.

Power Play

The player's power play (5v4, 5v3, 4v3, etc.) WAR, rated per 82 games (or 84 from 2026-2027 onwards) and weighted over the past 3 seasons. It's the player's impact on their team's expected goals for and against while on the power play, adjusted for teammates and opponents using regularized adjusted plus-minus (RAPM). Players without enough power play ice time (an average of 45 seconds per game played) do not qualify for a percentile, but still have a value. The small graph under it is the player's Power Play WAR percentile in each of the last five seasons (with the same y-axis grid lines as Overall WAR). To see how xG, RAPM, and WAR are calculated, check out the methodologies page.

Penalty Kill

The player's penalty kill (4v5, 3v5, 3v4, etc.) WAR, rated per 82 games (or 84 from 2026-2027 onwards) and weighted over the past 3 seasons. It's the player's impact on their team's expected goals for and against while on the penalty kill, adjusted for teammates and opponents using regularized adjusted plus-minus (RAPM). Players without enough penalty kill ice time (an average of 45 seconds per game played) do not qualify for a percentile, but still have a value. The small graph under it is the player's Penalty Kill WAR percentile in each of the last five seasons (with the same y-axis grid lines as Overall WAR). To see how xG, RAPM, and WAR are calculated, check out the methodologies page.

Finishing

The player's finishing WAR, for all situations, rated per 82 games (or 84 from 2026-2027 onwards) and weighted over the past 3 seasons. It's the player's goals scored above their expected goals (xG) on their own shots, compared with a replacement-level shooter. The small graph under it is the player's Finishing WAR percentile in each of the last five seasons (with the same y-axis grid lines as Overall WAR). To see how xG and WAR are calculated, check out the methodologies page.

Penalties

The player's penalties WAR, for all situations, rated per 82 games (or 84 from 2026-2027 onwards) and weighted over the past 3 seasons. It's the net penalty minutes the player drew versus took, valued by the expected goals those penalties are worth. The small graph under it is the player's Penalties WAR percentile in each of the last five seasons (with the same y-axis grid lines as Overall WAR). To see how xG and WAR are calculated, check out the methodologies page.

xGoals

The player's expected goals (xG) at 5v5, rated per 82 games (or 84 from 2026-2027 onwards) and weighted over the past 3 seasons. It's the player's total chance of scoring on their own unblocked shot attempts, based on where and how each was taken, measuring the quality of their shots. To see how xG is calculated, check out the methodologies page.

Goals

The player's goals scored at 5v5, rated per 82 games (or 84 from 2026-2027 onwards) and weighted over the past 3 seasons.

Shots

The player's shots on goal at 5v5, rated per 82 games (or 84 from 2026-2027 onwards) and weighted over the past 3 seasons. Shots on goal include goals and saved shots, but not missed shots or blocked shots.

Primary Assists

The player's primary assists at 5v5, rated per 82 games (or 84 from 2026-2027 onwards) and weighted over the past 3 seasons. A primary assist is the last pass before a goal. Secondary assists (the second last pass before a goal) are not counted.

Hits

The player's hits thrown at 5v5, rated per 82 games (or 84 from 2026-2027 onwards) and weighted over the past 3 seasons.

xGoal Share

The player's share of 5v5 expected goals (xG), weighted over the past 3 seasons. xG share is calculated as a player's on ice xG for divided by the sum of on ice xG for and on ice xG against. 50% is even. To see how xG is calculated, check out the methodologies page.

Goal Share

The player's share of 5v5 goals, weighted over the past 3 seasons. Goal share is calculated as a player's on ice goals for divided by the sum of on ice goals for and on ice goals against. 50% is even.

Shot Share

The player's share of 5v5 shots, weighted over the past 3 seasons. Shot share is calculated as a player's on ice shots for divided by the sum of on ice shots for and on ice shots against. 50% is even.

Shooting %

The player's team's shooting percentage (SH%) at 5v5 while the player was on the ice, weighted over the past 3 seasons. Shooting percentage is calculated as goals divided by shots on goal. A typical player will have a SH% + SV% of about 100 (much higher could mean lucky and much lower could mean unlucky).

Save %

The player's team's goalie save percentage (SV%) at 5v5 while the player was on the ice, weighted over the past 3 seasons. Save percentage is calculated as saves divided by shots on goal. A typical player will have a SH% + SV% of about 100 (much higher could mean lucky and much lower could mean unlucky).

5v5 Teammates

The player's quality of teammates (QoT), weighted over the past 3 seasons. QoT is calculated as the average even strength WAR of the player's teammates (offense and defense averaged), weighted by the 5v5 time they shared. To see how WAR is calculated, check out the methodologies page.

5v5 Opponents

The player's quality of competition (QoC), weighted over the past 3 seasons. QoC is calculated as the average even strength WAR of the player's opponents (offense and defense averaged), weighted by the 5v5 time they shared. To see how WAR is calculated, check out the methodologies page.

5v5 Ice Time

The player's average ice time per game at 5v5 (in minutes and seconds), weighted over the past 3 seasons.

5v4 Ice Time

The player's average ice time per game at 5v4 (in minutes and seconds), weighted over the past 3 seasons. Players without enough power play ice time (an average of 45 seconds per game played) do not qualify for a percentile, but still have a value.

4v5 Ice Time

The player's average ice time per game at 4v5 (in minutes and seconds), weighted over the past 3 seasons. Players without enough penalty kill ice time (an average of 45 seconds per game played) do not qualify for a percentile, but still have a value.

How to Read a Percentile Bar

Every stat has a name (the text on the left), the player's value (the number on the right), and a percentile bar. The percentile bar shows how the player ranks among qualified players in the same position group, using the same weighted seasons. The number on the bar is the percentile, which is the percentage of qualified players that the player ranks higher than. Stats where less is better, such as goals against, are flipped so a high percentile is always good. A gray bar means the player did not qualify for that stat's percentile (seen in special team stats).

Learn More About Each Section

Hover over or click/tap any part of the card to see what it means.

Profile

Information about who the goalie is. The position they play and their catching hand, their age (on September 1 of the given season's first year) and their birthday, their height (in feet and inches) and weight (in pounds), their draft position and year (or Undrafted), and birth country.

Season Stats

The goalie's stats from the given season only, in all situations (not weighted). Their games played (GP), record (wins-losses-overtime/shootout losses), save percentage (SV%), goals saved above expected (GSAx), and wins above replacement (WAR). These are different from the weighted numbers below.

Overall WAR

The goalie's total wins above replacement (WAR), for all situations, rated per 60 games and weighted over the past 3 seasons. It is the sum of the three components on the right. The graph below is the overall WAR percentile in each of the last five seasons, using that season alone (not weighted), with the logos showing the team(s) the goalie played for. A season without a dot means the goalie did not qualify. To see how xG and WAR are calculated, check out the methodologies page.

Even Strength

The goalie's even strength (5v5, 4v4, and 3v3) WAR, rated per 60 games and weighted over the past 3 seasons. It's the goals the goalie saved compared with what a replacement-level goalie would be expected to save facing the same shots. The small graph under it is the goalie's Even Strength WAR percentile in each of the last five seasons (with the same y-axis grid lines as Overall WAR). To see how GSAx and WAR are calculated, check out the methodologies page.

Penalty Kill

The goalie's penalty kill (4v5, 3v5, 3v4, etc.) WAR, rated per 60 games and weighted over the past 3 seasons. It's the goals the goalie saved while their team was short-handed compared with what a replacement-level goalie would be expected to save facing the same shots. The small graph under it is the goalie's Penalty Kill WAR percentile in each of the last five seasons (with the same y-axis grid lines as Overall WAR). To see how xG and WAR are calculated, check out the methodologies page.

Power Play

The goalie's power play (5v4, 5v3, 4v3, etc.) WAR, rated per 60 games and weighted over the past 3 seasons. It's the goals the goalie saved while their team had the man advantage compared with what a replacement-level goalie would be expected to save facing the same shots. The small graph under it is the goalie's Power Play WAR percentile in each of the last five seasons (with the same y-axis grid lines as Overall WAR). To see how xG and WAR are calculated, check out the methodologies page.

Save %

The goalie's save percentage (SV%) at 5v5, weighted over the past 3 seasons. Save percentage is calculated as saves divided by shots on goal.

GSAx

The goalie's goals saved above expected (GSAx) at 5v5, rated per 60 games and weighted over the past 3 seasons. GSAx is calculated as expected goals (xG) minus actual goals. Positive means they saved more than expected. To see how xG is calculated, check out the methodologies page.

LD GSAx

The goalie's goals saved above expected (GSAx) on low danger shots at 5v5, rated per 60 games and weighted over the past 3 seasons. To see how xG is calculated, check out the methodologies page. To see the low danger zone, check out the glossary.

MD GSAx

The goalie's goals saved above expected (GSAx) on medium danger shots at 5v5, rated per 60 games and weighted over the past 3 seasons. To see how xG is calculated, check out the methodologies page. To see the medium danger zone, check out the glossary.

HD GSAx

The goalie's goals saved above expected (GSAx) on high danger shots at 5v5, rated per 60 games and weighted over the past 3 seasons. To see how xG is calculated, check out the methodologies page. To see the high danger zone, check out the glossary.

Goals Against

The goalie's goals allowed at 5v5, rated per 60 games and weighted over the past 3 seasons.

xGoals Against

The goalie's expected goals against (xGA) at 5v5, rated per 60 games and weighted over the past 3 seasons. It's the total chance of scoring on the shots the goalie faced, measuring the quality of the shots they faced. To see how xG is calculated, check out the methodologies page.

LD Shots Against

The goalie's low danger shots on goal faced at 5v5, rated per 60 games and weighted over the past 3 seasons. To see the low danger zone, check out the glossary.

MD Shots Against

The goalie's medium danger shots on goal faced at 5v5, rated per 60 games and weighted over the past 3 seasons. To see the medium danger zone, check out the glossary.

HD Shots Against

The goalie's high danger shots on goal faced at 5v5, rated per 60 games and weighted over the past 3 seasons. To see the high danger zone, check out the glossary.

Game Start %

The percentage of the team's games the goalie started, weighted over the past 3 seasons.

Excellent Start %

The percentage of the goalie's starts with at least 1.5 goals saved above expected (GSAx), weighted over the past 3 seasons.

Quality Start %

The percentage of the goalie's starts with at least 0 goals saved above expected (GSAx), weighted over the past 3 seasons.

Bad Start %

The percentage of the goalie's starts with below 0 goals saved above expected (GSAx), weighted over the past 3 seasons.

Awful Start %

The percentage of the goalie's starts with equal to or below -1.5 goals saved above expected (GSAx), weighted over the past 3 seasons.

How to Read a Percentile Bar

Every stat has a name (the text on the left), the goalie's value (the number on the right), and a percentile bar. The percentile bar shows how the goalie ranks among qualified goalies, using the same weighted seasons. The number on the bar is the percentile, which is the percentage of qualified goalies that the goalie ranks higher than. Stats where less is better, such as goals against, are flipped so a high percentile is always good.
